### Ilyes M. Trad

Jurist building legal AI that can be checked — for the Gulf and France, in Arabic, French and English.
Judge since 2021; lecturer and doctoral researcher in law and artificial intelligence (MANAR University).

**[courtproof](https://github.com/Tradilyes/courtproof)** — checks for bilingual contracts (Arabic · English ·
French) that work with any AI model: clause-by-clause parity, references to laws repealed or renumbered
(UAE Civil Transactions Law, 1 June 2026), exact deadlines in Gregorian and Hijri calendars. Public
benchmark: on very long bilingual contracts (1,151 clauses and price rows), a small model with courtproof
found 60 of 60 planted discrepancies — as many as the strongest model tested, at a fifth of the cost; on
clauses citing the renumbered UAE law, 12 of 12 right where the models alone gave none.

**[compliance-atlas](https://github.com/Tradilyes/compliance-atlas)** — where client data may be hosted,
under which law, on what conditions: ten data-protection regimes of the Gulf and the EU, and the EU AI Act,
every fact with its source and date. On twenty questions about the rules in force on 1 November 2026, the
strongest model alone answered 11 right (and said a Kuwaiti breach notice was due in 72 hours; it is 24);
with the atlas, 20 of 20.

**[travail-fr](https://github.com/Tradilyes/travail-fr)** — French employment-litigation assistant for
Claude Code and Codex: verified official sources, local calculation tools, deadlines, drafting, an
independent reviewer, and a public demonstration bench.

Each project comes with playbooks a legal team can follow: a four-week pilot of bilingual contract review,
a template sweep after a law is renumbered, a hosting answer for a new AI vendor, the first 24 hours of a
data breach, due diligence on a legal-AI vendor, an AI-use policy.

I write with the help of AI assistants and publish the tests that check the result.
